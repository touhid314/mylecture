Hardware Security: The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad in Hardware:
1. Confidentiality ($C$): Protecting secret data (e.g., cryptographic keys) stored in hardware or traveling over buses from being read by unauthorized probes.
2. Integrity ($I$): Ensuring the hardware behaves exactly as designed and data hasn't been maliciously altered (e.g., via fault injection).
3. Availability ($A$): Ensuring the hardware remains operational and accessible, resisting Denial of Service (DoS) and physical sabotage.
