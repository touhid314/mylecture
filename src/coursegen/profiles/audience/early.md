# Course profile - early learners

audience: early learners and pre-readers/early readers. Use audio-first explanations, concrete examples, and very little on-screen text.

visual system:
- aspect ratio 16:9, dark charcoal background (#121212)
- very large labels (32px+), icons, simple scenes, and one focus item at a time
- topic-native visuals that can be understood before reading
- gentle reveal and pointing animations; avoid busy motion

teaching style:
- begin with a familiar situation or question, then name the skill in simple words
- one idea per scene; repeat important words consistently
- use short narration sentences and frequent examples
- end with one simple "you can now..." statement

pacing:
- about 105 words narration per minute
- use minimal written text; narration carries the explanation
