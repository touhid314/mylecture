# Course profile - graduate

audience: graduate students. Assume strong undergraduate preparation in the discipline named by the prompt.

visual system:
- aspect ratio 16:9, minimal dark theme (#121212)
- formal topic-native representations: frameworks, derivations, models, mechanisms, evidence chains, architectures, close readings, simulations, or case structures as appropriate
- use advanced notation and domain conventions only when standard for the subject
- dense but legible layout; highlight assumptions, invariants, constraints, and boundary conditions

teaching style:
- begin by naming the specific problem, result, or interpretive question the segment resolves
- develop key results or arguments; do not hand-wave steps
- state prerequisites through disciplined terminology, not remedial tutorials
- emphasize mechanisms, approximations, interpretations, and failure modes
- pace: 5-8 minute lectures

pacing:
- about 155 words narration per minute
- prefer precision over analogy; one deep insight per segment
