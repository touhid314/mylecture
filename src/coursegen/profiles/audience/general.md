# Course profile - general audience

audience: curious adults and self-learners with no assumed background. Explain in plain English; avoid jargon unless defined.

visual system:
- aspect ratio 16:9, polished dark course style (#121212)
- explanatory visuals chosen from the topic: processes, comparisons, timelines, maps, systems, artifacts, examples, before/after states, or cause-effect chains
- elegant line illustrations; accent color per topic
- subtle motion that supports the explanation; change visuals when the lesson structure changes, not for novelty

teaching style:
- open with a concrete question, problem, or motivating example that makes the lesson goal useful
- orient the learner before going deep: what they will learn, why it matters, and the first example
- teach through story where it helps: a real situation, a person facing the problem, or a before/after contrast - then extract the idea from it
- build toward one clear learning objective, not a broad survey
- pace: 3-6 minute lectures

pacing:
- about 140 words narration per minute
- end with a practical takeaway or next-step question
